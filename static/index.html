Data & Privacy Notice

This mock interviewer uses free open-source LLMs via OpenRouter to run the interview; your voice is transcribed locally on the server (faster-whisper).

The job description and your resume are sent to OpenRouter to generate questions and feedback. No data is stored permanently.

By continuing you accept this. More details at openrouter.ai.

How it works

  1. Share the job — paste or upload the job description and your resume.
  2. Pick an interviewer — choose who you'll be interviewed by.
  3. Get interviewed — a natural, sectioned interview, one question at a time, with follow-ups.
  4. See your scorecard — a 0–100 score with strengths and areas to improve.
Your voice is transcribed locally and the interview is powered by free open-source LLMs (OpenRouter). Nothing is stored permanently.
